📐 Beating the Sportsbook on Value (+EV vs. Single-Game Variance)
The Coin Flip Principle
In professional sports betting, beating the sportsbook is defined by positive expected value (+EV), NOT by the random result of a single 9-inning game.

Imagine a friend offers you a deal on a 50/50 coin flip: if it lands on Tails, you lose $10; if it lands on Heads, you win $15. That is a +25% EV bet.
· If the coin lands on Tails and you lose $10, did you make a mistake? NO. You still executed a +EV wager.
· If you repeat that coin flip 100 times, math guarantees you walk away with +$250 in net profit.

A single baseball game has random noise (a blown save, a bad umpire call, a soft bloop hit). When you place a bet with a +4.2% EV edge, you beat the sportsbook on value the moment your bet is placed · regardless of whether that single game wins or loses.
📊 What If the "Best Bet" Shifts Later?
If you bet Yankees ML (+4.2% EV) at 2 hours pre-game, and at 30 minutes pre-game the single "Best Bet" banner shifts to OVER 9.0 (+6.0% EV):

1. ML and O/U are TWO SEPARATE markets: They are not mutually exclusive. A game ending Yankees 7·4 wins BOTH the Yankees ML bet AND the OVER 9.0 bet!
2. Your initial bet remains +EV: Your +4.2% ML bet is still mathematically profitable. The model simply found a second market with a slightly higher gap due to late weather/lineup updates.
3. Best Action: Keep your ML bet. Optionally, add the OVER 9.0 as a 2nd independent bet if your bankroll permits. Never cash out early · sportsbooks charge heavy cash-out juice that destroys your long-term ROI.

🧠 Betting Concepts: EV, CLV & Signals
What is EV (Expected Value) and why does it matter? ·
Expected Value (EV) measures the theoretical profit or loss per bet over a large sample size.

EV = (Model Win Probability ? Sportsbook Payout) - (Loss Probability ? Wager)

A bet with +EV means the sportsbook is offering higher odds than the true mathematical probability of the event occurring. Placing only +EV bets guarantees long-term statistical profitability regardless of short-term variance.
What is CLV (Closing Line Value)? ·
Closing Line Value (CLV) compares the odds you placed against the final line right before first pitch. If you bet Braves at +125 and the line closes at +105, you achieved positive CLV. Beating the closing line consistently is the gold standard indicator of sharp, profitable long-term sports betting.
What do PRIME, EDGE, and SKIP signals mean? ·
· 🔥 PRIME: Top-tier mathematical advantage (historically 57%+ hit rate on ML, EV +3.5% to +11.0%). High-confidence sweet spot.
· ✅ EDGE: Positive expected value (+1.0% to +3.5% EV). Solid value spot worth tracking.
· ⬜ SKIP: No mathematical edge detected or line is tight. The model strongly advises passing on this market.
What is Edge %? ·
Edge % is the difference between our model's win probability and the sportsbook's implied probability. For example, if our model projects a team at 56.0% (implied odds -127) and DraftKings prices them at +100 (50.0% implied), your mathematical edge is +6.0%.
